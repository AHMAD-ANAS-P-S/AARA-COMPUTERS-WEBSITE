About AARA Computers

Organizational Profile

Since 2012, a legacy of excellence

Since 2012, Aara Computers has been a trusted academy and information technology organization providing quality education and technology solutions.

We are one of Chennai's leading sales and service providers of branded, new, and refurbished computers, laptops, desktops, servers, workstations, CCTV systems, and IT products.

Our mission is to provide affordable, practical, and career-focused education while delivering reliable IT services for businesses and individuals.
